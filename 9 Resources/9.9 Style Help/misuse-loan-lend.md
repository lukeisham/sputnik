# Loan vs. Lend

## The Distinction

*Lend* is a verb. *Loan* is a noun. In formal writing, avoid using *loan* as a verb.

✅ Would you **lend** me your pen?
❌ Would you **loan** me your pen?

✅ I applied for a bank **loan**.
✅ The **loan** was approved.

## Past Tense

The past tense of *lend* is *lent*:

✅ She **lent** me her car.
❌ She **loaned** me her car.

## Modern Usage Note

In American English, *loan* as a verb is now common in both speech and informal writing. In British English and formal writing, the distinction remains stricter. For the most polished prose, keep *lend* and *loan* in their separate roles.

✅ The gallery agreed to **lend** the painting for the exhibition. (preferred)
⚠️ The gallery agreed to **loan** the painting... (informal, American)
