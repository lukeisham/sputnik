# Interesting

## A Perfunctory Introduction

Avoid *interesting* as a lazy way to introduce a topic. Instead of announcing that what you are about to tell is interesting, make it so.

✅ Tell the story without preamble.
❌ An **interesting** story is told of...

✅ Mr. B., who is expected to visit America soon, once...
❌ In connection with Mr. B.'s anticipated visit, it is **interesting** to recall that he...

## Why It Weakens Writing

Prefacing a fact with "it is interesting that" is the writer's way of saying, "I hope you find this interesting." If the fact is interesting, it will speak for itself. If it is not, no amount of labelling will help.

## Legitimate Uses

*Interesting* is fine when genuinely describing a reaction rather than advertising one:

✅ I found the documentary **interesting**.
✅ She raised an **interesting** question about the budget.

## The Test

If you are using *interesting* to signal to the reader that they should pay attention, delete it and let the content do the work.
