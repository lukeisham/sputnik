# Thanking You in Advance

## Avoid This Phrase

"Thanking you in advance" sounds as if the writer means, "It will not be worth my while to write to you again." It presumes the reader's compliance and forecloses on gratitude.

✅ Will you please send the files by Friday?
❌ **Thanking you in advance** for sending the files by Friday.

## What to Use Instead

Make your request directly and politely. If a further acknowledgement is warranted, send a separate thank-you note afterward.

✅ I would appreciate it if you could send the files by Friday.
✅ I shall be obliged if you would forward the documents.
✅ Could you please send the files by Friday? Thank you.

## The Principle

Gratitude should follow the favour, not precede it. Pre-emptive thanks can read as presumptuous. Reserve your thanks for when the request has been fulfilled.
