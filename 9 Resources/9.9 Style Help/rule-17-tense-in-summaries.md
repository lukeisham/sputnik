# In Summaries, Keep to One Tense

When summarizing a drama, always use the present tense. When summarizing a poem, story, or novel, the present tense is preferred, though the past tense is acceptable. Whatever tense you choose, stick to it throughout.

## Key Points

- **Drama** → always present tense.
- **Fiction and poetry** → present tense preferred; past acceptable.
- **Antecedent action** → if using present, use present perfect for earlier events; if using past, use past perfect.
- **Indirect discourse** → past tense in indirect speech or questions remains past, regardless of the summary tense.
- **Avoid tense shifting** — shifting between past and present suggests uncertainty and irresolution.
