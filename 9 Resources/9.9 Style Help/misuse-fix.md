# Fix

## Colloquial vs. Literary Uses

*Fix* is colloquial in America for *arrange*, *prepare*, or *mend*. In writing, restrict it to its literary senses: *fasten*, *make firm or immovable*, and the like.

✅ The bracket must be **fixed** to the wall with screws. (fastened)
✅ They **fixed** the date for the hearing. (set firmly)

❌ I need to **fix** dinner. (Use *prepare*)
❌ She **fixed** the broken lamp. (Use *repaired* or *mended*)
❌ Can you **fix** this for me? (Use *arrange* or *solve*)

## Exceptions

Some figurative uses are standard:

✅ She **fixed** her gaze on the horizon.
✅ He **fixed** the blame on his predecessor.

## The Test

If you could substitute *fasten* or *make firm* and the meaning holds, *fix* is correct. If you mean *repair*, *prepare*, or *arrange*, choose the more precise word.
