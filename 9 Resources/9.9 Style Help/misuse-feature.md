# Feature

## Another Hackneyed Word

Like *factor*, *feature* is overused and typically adds nothing to the sentence.

✅ Miss A.'s singing of Schubert lieder was especially fine.
❌ A **feature** of the entertainment especially worthy of mention was the singing of Miss A.

## Write the Specifics

Instead of labelling something "a feature," describe what actually happened. If the programme has already been given, tell *how* she sang, not merely *that* she sang.

## As a Verb

Avoid *feature* as a verb in the advertising sense of "offer as a special attraction."

❌ The restaurant **features** locally sourced ingredients.
✅ The restaurant uses locally sourced ingredients.

## Legitimate Uses

*Feature* is correct when referring to a distinctive attribute in a precise context:

✅ The new phone's standout **feature** is its battery life.
✅ Facial **features**; geographical **features**
