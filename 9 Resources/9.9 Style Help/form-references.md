# Citations and References

## Abbreviate Frequent Titles

In scholarly work requiring exact references, abbreviate titles that occur frequently. Provide the full forms in an alphabetical list at the end of the work.

## Placement

Give references in parentheses or in footnotes — not in the body of the sentence.

✅ Hamlet is placed under guard (IV.ii.14).
❌ In the fourth act, second scene, line fourteen, Hamlet is placed under guard.

## Omit Redundant Labels

Omit the words *act*, *scene*, *line*, *book*, *volume*, and *page* except when referring by only one of them.

✅ In III.ii
❌ In the second scene of the third act

## Punctuation Examples

✅ *2 Samuel* i:17–27
✅ *Othello* II.iii.264–267, III.iii.155–161

## Modern Practice

Today's style guides (MLA, APA, Chicago) offer specific citation formats. Strunk's core principle endures: keep references concise, place them in parentheses or footnotes, and avoid cluttering the sentence body with bibliographic details.
