# Factor

## A Hackneyed Word

*Factor* is overused. The expressions in which it appears can almost always be replaced by something more direct and idiomatic.

✅ He won the match by being better trained.
❌ His superior training was the great **factor** in his winning the match.

✅ Heavy artillery has played a constantly larger part in deciding battles.
❌ Heavy artillery has become an increasingly important **factor** in deciding battles.

## Legitimate Uses

*Factor* has proper uses in mathematics, science, and precise contexts:

✅ The prime **factors** of 12 are 2 and 3.
✅ Sunscreen is a key **factor** in preventing skin cancer.

## The Test

If *factor* is preceded by a vague adjective (*important*, *key*, *major*, *significant*), try cutting the phrase entirely or replacing it with a concrete subject.

❌ Cost was a key **factor** in the decision.
✅ Cost drove the decision.
