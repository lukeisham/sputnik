# However

## Position in the Sentence

When *however* means *nevertheless*, it should not come first in its sentence or clause.

✅ The roads were almost impassable. At last, **however**, we succeeded in reaching camp.
❌ The roads were almost impassable. **However**, we at last succeeded in reaching camp.

## When *However* Comes First

When *however* comes first, it means *in whatever way* or *to whatever extent* — not *nevertheless*.

✅ **However** you advise him, he will probably do as he thinks best.
✅ **However** discouraging the prospect, he never lost heart.

## Placement Options

When meaning *nevertheless*, embed *however* after the first element or near the start:

✅ The work is difficult. It is, **however**, essential.
✅ She disagreed. Her colleague, **however**, supported the plan.

The best position is after the word or phrase that contrasts most strongly with what came before.
