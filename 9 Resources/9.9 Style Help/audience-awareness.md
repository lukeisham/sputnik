# Audience Awareness

Every piece of writing is a conversation with a specific reader. The writer who does not know their audience writes into a void — and the void rarely writes back. Audience shapes everything: diction, structure, what you explain, and what you assume.

## Key Rules

1. **Assess What They Know** — A specialist needs technical precision and data. A general reader needs context and translation. Never assume, but don't patronise either.
2. **Assess What They Need** — Is the reader looking for a decision, a recommendation, an explanation, or a story? Match your structure to their goal.
3. **Shift Diction and Tone** — The same idea expressed to a CEO, a peer reviewer, and a high school class uses different vocabulary, sentence length, and formality.
4. **Bridge the Context Gap** — Define terms the audience may not know, but skip definitions they already own. Guessing wrong in either direction loses the reader.

✅ *For a technical audience:* The API returns a 429 status when the rate limit is exceeded.
✅ *For a non-technical audience:* When too many requests arrive at once, the system temporarily blocks new ones and asks you to wait.
