# Form the Possessive Singular of Nouns by Adding 's

Follow this rule whatever the final consonant. The possessive singular is formed by adding _'s_ — even when the noun ends in _s_.

## Key Points

- Add _'s_ to all singular nouns, regardless of their final letter.
- Avoid awkward constructions by rewording if the result sounds clumsy.

✅ Charles's friend
✅ Burns's poems
✅ the witch's malice

The exceptions are few: ancient proper names ending in _-es_ or _-is_ (_Jesus'_, _Moses' laws_), and the fixed phrases _for conscience' sake_ and _for righteousness' sake_. When in doubt, recast: _the laws of Moses_ instead of _Moses' laws_.

Remember that pronominal possessives — _hers_, _its_, _theirs_, _yours_, _oneself_ — never take an apostrophe.

❌ Charles' friend
❌ Burn's poems
❌ The cat licked it's paw.
