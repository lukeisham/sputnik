# People vs. Persons

## The Distinction

*The people* is a political term. *The public* refers to artistic appreciation or commercial patronage. These should not be confused.

✅ From **the people** comes political support or opposition.
✅ From **the public** comes artistic appreciation or commercial patronage.

## People vs. Persons

In modern usage, *people* is the normal plural of *person* for most contexts. *Persons* is reserved for formal, legal, or technical contexts:

✅ How many **people** attended the concert?
✅ The elevator has a capacity of eight **persons**. (legal/technical)
✅ The police are searching for a missing **person**.

## Strunk's Specific Point

*The people* carries a political, collective connotation that *the public* does not. A politician appeals to *the people*; an artist seeks recognition from *the public*.

✅ The referendum gave **the people** a direct voice.
✅ The exhibition was well received by **the public**.
