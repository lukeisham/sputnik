# Unique

## Means "One of a Kind"

*Unique* means "the only one of its kind." It is an absolute: something is either unique or it is not. It cannot be modified by *very*, *more*, *most*, *rather*, or *somewhat*.

✅ The manuscript is **unique**.
❌ The manuscript is **very unique**.
❌ That is a **rather unique** perspective.

## Common Misuse

❌ It was the **most unique** experience of my life.
(Something cannot be "most one-of-a-kind.")

✅ It was the most extraordinary experience of my life.
✅ It was a **unique** experience. (If it truly had no parallel.)

## Acceptable Alternatives

When you mean *unusual*, *distinctive*, or *remarkable*, use those words:

| Intended Meaning | Better Word |
|---|---|
| very unusual | extraordinary, remarkable |
| quite distinctive | unusual, striking |
| rather special | exceptional, singular |

## The Only Exception

Some usage authorities accept *unique* as meaning simply "very unusual" in informal contexts. In formal writing, preserve the absolute sense.
