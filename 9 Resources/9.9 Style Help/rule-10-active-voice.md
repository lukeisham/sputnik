# Use the Active Voice

The active voice is more direct, vigorous, and concise than the passive. It makes clear who is doing what.

## Key Points

- **Prefer active voice** — Active sentences are shorter, clearer, and more engaging.
- **When passive works** — Use passive when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or when the receiver of the action should be the focus.
- **Identify passive** — Look for a form of _to be_ + past participle (_was written_, _is being built_).
- **Avoid stacked passives** — Never make one passive depend on another (_He has been proved to have been seen..._).
- **Weak "there is" and "could be heard"** — Replace with an active verb for a livelier sentence.

✅ I shall always remember my first visit to Boston.
✅ Dead leaves covered the ground.
✅ Somewhere in the house a guitar hummed sleepily.
✅ Failing health compelled him to leave college.
✅ He soon repented his words.

❌ My first visit to Boston will always be remembered by me.
❌ There were a great number of dead leaves lying on the ground.
❌ The sound of a guitar somewhere in the house could be heard.
❌ The reason that he left college was that his health became impaired.
❌ The experiment was conducted by the researcher.
❌ The decision was made by the committee.
