# System

## Frequently Used Without Need

*System* is often tacked onto words where it adds nothing.

✅ Dayton has adopted government by commission.
❌ Dayton has adopted the commission **system** of government.

✅ Dormitories
❌ The dormitory **system**

## Legitimate Uses

*System* is correct when it refers to an organised, interconnected set of components:

✅ The solar **system**
✅ The nervous **system**
✅ A new filing **system**

## The Test

If you can delete *system* and the meaning holds, delete it.

❌ The company implemented a new bonus **system**.
✅ The company implemented a new bonus **structure**. (if structure is what is meant)
✅ The company changed how bonuses are calculated. (better yet — be specific)
