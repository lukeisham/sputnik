# Express Co-ordinate Ideas in Similar Form

Parallel structure means using the same grammatical form for items in a series, pairs, or comparisons. Likeness of form enables the reader to recognize likeness of content.

## Key Points

- Elements joined by _and_, _or_, _but_ or correlative pairs (_both...and_, _either...or_, _neither...nor_, _not only...but also_) must share the same grammatical construction.
- Articles and prepositions in a series should either appear only before the first term or be repeated before each term.
- In comparisons, match the grammatical form on both sides.
- Bullet lists should start each item with the same part of speech.

✅ Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method; now it is taught by the laboratory method.
✅ The ceremony was both long and tedious.
✅ You must either grant his request or incur his ill will.
✅ My objections are, first, that the measure is unjust; second, that it is unconstitutional.
✅ The French, the Italians, the Spanish, and the Portuguese.
✅ In spring, summer, or winter.
✅ She likes running, swimming, and cycling.
✅ The job involves typing, filing, and answering phones.

❌ Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method, while now the laboratory method is employed.
❌ It was both a long ceremony and very tedious.
❌ A time not for words, but action.
❌ Either you must grant his request or incur his ill will.
❌ My objections are, first, the injustice of the measure; second, that it is unconstitutional.
❌ She likes running, swimming, and to cycle.
❌ The job involves typing, filing, and to answer phones.
