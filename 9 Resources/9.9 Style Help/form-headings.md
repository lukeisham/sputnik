# Headings and Manuscript Formatting

## Leave Space After Headings

Leave a blank line — or equivalent spacing — after the title or heading of a manuscript. On succeeding pages, if using ruled paper, begin on the first line.

This rule ensures visual separation between the heading and the body text, making the structure clear at a glance.

## Modern Application

In word processors and plain-text editors, insert a blank line between your heading and the first paragraph:

```
# Chapter Title

The text begins here, separated by one blank line.
```

✅ Clear separation between heading and body
❌ No blank line — the heading runs into the text
