# One of the Most

## A Threadbare Opener

Avoid beginning essays or paragraphs with "One of the most interesting developments of modern science is..." or "Switzerland is one of the most interesting countries of Europe." There is nothing grammatically wrong with the construction — it is simply threadbare and forcible-feeble.

## The Singular Verb Trap

A common error is using a singular verb in a relative clause following "one of the" when the relative pronoun is the subject.

✅ One of the ablest men **that have** attacked this problem.
❌ One of the ablest men **that has** attacked this problem.

The logic: *that* refers to *men* (plural), not to *one*. It is the men who have attacked the problem.

## Fresh Alternatives

Instead of the formulaic "one of the most," state the claim directly:

❌ One of the most significant developments in modern science is CRISPR.
✅ CRISPR has transformed genetic research.

❌ She is one of the most accomplished pianists of her generation.
✅ Few pianists of her generation match her technique and range.
