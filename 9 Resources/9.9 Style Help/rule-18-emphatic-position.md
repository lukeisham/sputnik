# Place the Emphatic Words of a Sentence at the End

The most prominent position in a sentence is the end. The word or group of words the writer wants to emphasize — usually the new element, the logical predicate — belongs there.

## Key Points

- **End with emphasis** — save the most important point for the final position.
- **The beginning is also emphatic** — any element other than the subject gains emphasis when placed first.
- **Periodic sentences** — sentences that delay the main clause until the end — derive their power from this principle.
- A subject at the start is not automatically emphatic; the closing position carries more weight.

✅ Humanity, since that time, has advanced in many other ways, but it has hardly advanced in fortitude.
✅ Because of its hardness, this steel is principally used in making razors.
✅ Deceit or treachery he could never forgive.

❌ Humanity has hardly advanced in fortitude since that time, though it has advanced in many other ways.
❌ This steel is principally used for making razors, because of its hardness.

The periodic sentence illustrates the principle perfectly: _Four centuries ago, Christopher Columbus, one of the Italian mariners...seeking for Spain a westward passage...lighted on America._ &nbsp;— all the energy builds toward the final revelation.
