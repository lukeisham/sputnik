# State

## Not a Synonym for *Say*

*State* should not be used as a mere substitute for *say* or *remark*. Restrict it to the sense of *express fully or clearly*.

✅ He refused to **state** his objections.
(He gave them clearly and formally — *state* fits.)

❌ "I'll be there at noon," he **stated**.
✅ "I'll be there at noon," he **said**.

## When *State* Is Correct

Use *state* when the act involves formality, precision, or laying out a position:

✅ The witness **stated** her name and address.
✅ The report **states** that emissions have fallen by 12%.
✅ The constitution **states** the rights of citizens.

## When *Said* Is Better

In narrative and dialogue, *said* is almost always the right choice. It is invisible to the reader. *Stated*, *remarked*, *opined*, and similar verbs draw attention to themselves.

✅ "I disagree," she **said**.
❌ "I disagree," she **stated**.
