# Begin Each Paragraph with a Topic Sentence

As a rule, open each paragraph with a sentence that states its central idea. The body of the paragraph then explains, develops, or proves that idea, and the final sentence either reinforces it or states an important consequence.

## Key Points

- The topic sentence lets the reader grasp the paragraph's purpose immediately.
- The body sentences support the topic sentence through restatement, illustration, proof, or development.
- End with emphasis — never with a digression or an unimportant detail.

✅ **Topic sentence.** The meaning is then made clearer by denial of the contrary.
✅ **Topic sentence.** The definition is expanded. The definition is explained by contrast. Conclusion: an important consequence.

In narrative and description, the opening sentence may instead hold together the details that follow (_The breeze served us admirably._), but in exposition and argument, a clear topic sentence is indispensable.

❌ A paragraph that opens with a minor detail, wanders through supporting facts, and trails off without a concluding emphasis.
