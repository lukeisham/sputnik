# Further vs Farther

In British English, the two words have largely merged in casual use, but American English and careful writing everywhere preserve a distinction. It is a distinction worth keeping, because it maps neatly onto two different kinds of distance.

## Key Rules

1. **Farther** — Physical, measurable distance. If you can measure it in miles, metres, or inches, use *farther*.
2. **Further** — Figurative degree, extent, or advancement. Use *further* for anything non-physical: *further discussion, further study, without further delay, further to your letter*.
3. **When in Doubt** — If you cannot decide, *further* is the safer default because it is acceptable in more contexts, particularly in British English.

✅ The next gas station is three miles farther down the road.
✅ We need further discussion before making a decision.

❌ The next gas station is three miles further down the road. *(prefer farther for physical distance)*
❌ We need farther discussion before making a decision.

## Memory Aid

*Farther* contains *far* — think of *how far?* If the answer involves distance, use *farther*. For everything else, use *further*.
