# In a Series of Three or More Terms, Use a Comma After Each Term Except the Last

In a list joined by a single conjunction (_and_, _or_), place a comma after every item except the final one. This is the Oxford (or serial) comma.

## Key Points

- Always place a comma before the final _and_ or _or_ in a series of three or more.
- The serial comma prevents ambiguity and clarifies that the last two items are distinct.

✅ red, white, and blue
✅ gold, silver, or copper
✅ He opened the letter, read it, and made a note of its contents.

Omitting the final comma can create confusion — the reader may momentarily group the last two items together as a pair. 

❌ The flag is red, white and blue.
❌ We invited the parrots, Alice and Bob. &nbsp;_(Are Alice and Bob the parrots?)_
