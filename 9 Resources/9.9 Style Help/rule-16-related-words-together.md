# Keep Related Words Together

The position of words in a sentence shows their relationship. Bring together words that are related in thought, and keep apart those that are not.

## Key Points

- **Subject and verb together** — Don't let a long phrase separate the subject from its verb unnecessarily. Move the interrupting phrase to the beginning.
- **Relative pronoun next to its antecedent** — _who_, _which_, _that_ should immediately follow the noun they refer to.
- **Modifiers next to the word modified** — misplaced _only_, _not_, and similar modifiers change meaning.
- **Unscramble the sentence** — If several modifying phrases hang off the same word, arrange them so that no wrong relationship is suggested.

✅ In the fifth book of _The Excursion_, Wordsworth gives a minute description of this church.
✅ He published in Harper's Magazine three articles about his adventures in Spain.
✅ Not all the members were present.
✅ He found only two mistakes.

❌ Wordsworth, in the fifth book of _The Excursion_, gives a minute description of this church.
❌ He wrote three articles about his adventures in Spain, which were published in Harper's Magazine.
❌ All the members were not present.
❌ He only found two mistakes.
❌ Major Joyce will give a lecture on Tuesday evening in Bailey Hall, to which the public is invited, on "My Experiences" at eight P.M.
