# Possess

## Not a Synonym for *Have* or *Own*

*Possess* should not be used as a mere substitute for *have* or *own*. It carries a weight that simple possession does not always warrant.

✅ He **had** great courage. (or: He was very brave.)
❌ He **possessed** great courage.

✅ He **owned** a large estate.
❌ He was the fortunate **possessor** of a large estate.

## When *Possess* Is Correct

Use *possess* when the connotation of full ownership, control, or a defining quality is intended:

✅ She **possesses** a rare combination of intellect and empathy.
✅ The painting is **possessed** by the museum.
✅ He was **possessed** by an idea.

## The Test

If *have* or *own* works, prefer it. Reserve *possess* for emphasis — when the act of possession itself carries weight.

❌ The company **possesses** three offices in the city.
✅ The company **has** three offices in the city.
