# Do Not Break Sentences in Two

In other words, do not use periods where commas belong. A sentence fragment — a phrase or dependent clause punctuated as a complete sentence — leaves the reader hanging.

## Key Points

- Replace the stray period with a comma and continue the sentence.
- Intentional fragments are permissible only for deliberate emphasis (e.g., _Again and again he called out. No reply._), and only when the emphasis is clearly warranted.

✅ I met them on a Cunard liner several years ago, coming home from Liverpool to New York.
✅ He was an interesting talker, a man who had traveled all over the world and lived in half a dozen countries.

❌ I met them on a Cunard liner several years ago. Coming home from Liverpool to New York.
❌ He was an interesting talker. A man who had traveled all over the world and lived in half a dozen countries.
