# Type

## Avoid Vague Use

*Type* is often used vaguely where a more specific word or no word at all is needed. Like *kind of* and *sort of*, it should not be used as a filler.

✅ I dislike that **type of** architecture.
(The literal sense — a category — is correct.)

❌ It was a protest **type** situation.
✅ It was a protest.

❌ He's the artistic **type**.
✅ He is artistic.

## Legitimate Uses

*Type* is correct when referring to a classification, category, or model:

✅ blood **type**; personality **type**; file **type**
✅ The building is a fine example of the Gothic **type**.

## Not a Substitute for *Kind of*

In colloquial use, *type* sometimes replaces *kind of* as a vague qualifier. Avoid this in writing:

❌ She was **type of** annoyed.
✅ She was somewhat annoyed.

## The Test

If you can delete "type" or "type of" without losing meaning, delete it.
