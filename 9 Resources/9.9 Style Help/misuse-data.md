# Data

## Data Is Plural

*Data* is a plural noun, like *phenomena* and *strata*. The singular is *datum*.

✅ These **data** were tabulated.
❌ This **data** was tabulated.

✅ The **data** show a clear trend.
❌ The **data** shows a clear trend.

## Modern Usage

In everyday and business writing, *data* is widely treated as a singular mass noun ("the data is conclusive"). This is now accepted in many style guides for general contexts.

In formal academic and scientific writing, however, the plural treatment remains preferred.

## Recommendation

- **Scientific and academic writing:** Treat as plural.
- **General and business writing:** Either is acceptable; be consistent.
- **When in doubt:** Use the plural — it is never wrong.

✅ The data **are** inconclusive. (formal)
✅ The data **is** inconclusive. (informal, but widespread)
