# The Fact That

## A Wordy Formula

The phrase *the fact that* is usually unnecessary. It adds words without adding meaning. Whenever possible, recast the sentence to eliminate it.

✅ I was unaware that she had arrived.
❌ I was unaware of **the fact that** she had arrived.

✅ Because he was late, we started without him.
❌ Owing to **the fact that** he was late, we started without him.

✅ Despite his injury, he finished the race.
❌ In spite of **the fact that** he was injured, he finished the race.

## When *Fact* Is Correct

Use *fact* only for matters capable of direct verification — not matters of judgment.

✅ It is a **fact** that lead melts at 327.5 °C.
✅ The **fact** that the meeting was recorded is not disputed.

❌ It is a **fact** that Napoleon was the greatest of modern generals.
(That is a judgment, not a verifiable fact.)

## Quick Repairs

| Wordy | Concise |
|---|---|
| due to the fact that | because |
| in spite of the fact that | although, despite |
| the fact that she had | her having |
| call attention to the fact that | note that, point out that |
