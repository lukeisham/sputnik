# Numerals

## Write Dates and Serial Numbers in Figures

Do not spell out dates or other serial numbers. Write them in figures or Roman notation, as appropriate.

✅ August 9, 1918 (or 9 August 1918)
❌ August ninth, nineteen eighteen

✅ Rule 3
❌ Rule three

✅ Chapter XII
❌ Chapter twelve

✅ 352nd Infantry
❌ Three hundred and fifty-second Infantry

## When to Spell Out

Spell out numbers that begin a sentence, small numbers in general prose, and round numbers used casually. The key distinction: serial numbers, dates, and designations stay in figures.

✅ Twelve people attended the meeting.
✅ She turned to page 342.
